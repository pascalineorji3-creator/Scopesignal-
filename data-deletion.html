User Data Deletion Instructions

ScopeSignal respects your privacy. This page explains how to request deletion of personal information associated with your use of ScopeSignal.

How to request deletion

Send an email to Orjipascaline1@gmail.com with the subject line ScopeSignal Data Deletion Request.

In your request, describe the ScopeSignal account or information you want deleted. Please do not email passwords or other sensitive authentication details.

What happens next?

We will review your request, contact you if reasonably necessary to verify it, and explain the next steps. We will delete applicable personal data that we control, subject to any legal obligations or legitimate requirements to retain certain records.

Until ScopeSignal provides a self-service deletion feature, email is the available method for submitting a deletion request.

Third-party accounts

If you connect a Facebook or Instagram account in the future, you can also remove ScopeSignal's access through the relevant Meta account settings. Revoking access does not necessarily delete information already stored by ScopeSignal; contact us to request deletion of that information.

Contact

For questions about data deletion, email Orjipascaline1@gmail.com.
