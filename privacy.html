SCOPESIGNAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

ScopeSignal is a tool designed to help freelancers and service providers compare client requests with project agreements, identify potential scope changes, and prepare professional responses. This policy explains how information is handled when you use ScopeSignal.

1. Information you provide

Depending on how you use the current application, information you enter may include project agreements, deliverables, revision limits, deadlines, fees, exclusions, client requests, and draft responses. Please avoid entering passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information.

2. How the current application stores information

The current ScopeSignal web application stores saved agreement information and review history in your browser's local storage. This means the information is stored locally in the browser on the device you use, rather than automatically being uploaded to a ScopeSignal server by the current application's local-storage features.

People with access to your device or browser profile may be able to access locally stored information. Clearing browser data or changing devices may remove or make that information unavailable. You can clear the relevant browser storage to remove locally stored application data.

3. How information is used

Information entered into the current application is used to compare a client request against the agreement details you provide, identify possible differences, prepare draft responses, and display or export review history at your request.

The current rules-based review features do not, by themselves, send your agreement or request text to an AI service. If future versions introduce cloud processing, AI providers, account services, or integrations, this policy must be updated to explain those data flows before they are enabled.

4. Facebook and Instagram integrations

ScopeSignal is being prepared for potential integrations with Meta services. Do not assume that Facebook or Instagram accounts are currently connected or that ScopeSignal currently reads their messages. Any future integration will depend on the relevant Meta permissions, user authorization, technical implementation, and applicable approval requirements.

If integrations are introduced, the policy will explain what information is accessed, why it is used, how it is retained, and how access can be revoked. You may also manage or revoke third-party app access through the relevant platform's settings.

5. Sharing and disclosure

The current local-storage features do not automatically transmit your saved agreement or review history to other people. You may choose to export or share information yourself. We do not claim that information is sent to external service providers unless a feature actually performs that transfer.

Information may need to be disclosed if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

6. Data security and retention

ScopeSignal aims to handle information responsibly. However, no website or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Because the current application stores some information in your browser, you are responsible for protecting access to your device and browser profile.

Locally stored information generally remains in your browser until you delete it or the browser removes it. Retention practices for any future server-side data will be explained when such features are implemented.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You can clear locally stored ScopeSignal data through your browser's site-data or storage settings. You can also request assistance with personal data by emailing Orjipascaline1@gmail.com.

For instructions, visit our User Data Deletion page.

8. Children's privacy

ScopeSignal is a professional productivity tool and is not designed to target children. Please do not submit children's personal information through the application.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as ScopeSignal develops. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. The policy should be reviewed whenever new integrations or data-processing features are introduced.
