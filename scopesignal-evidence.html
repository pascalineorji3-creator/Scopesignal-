1. Choose screenshots

Select multiple JPEG, PNG, or WebP screenshots, up to 10 MB each. OCR runs in your browser. Clear, high-resolution screenshots usually work best.

2. Review extracted conversation

OCR can miss messages or misread names, punctuation, timestamps, and small text. Check the text against the original screenshots and correct it before using it for a review.

Your reviewed text stays on this page until you copy or save it.

3. Save evidence privately to your account

Sign in first. Original screenshots, the reviewed transcript, and a small batch record are uploaded to the private scopesignal-evidence bucket under your account folder. No public links are created.

Privacy and limits

Evidence is stored in private user folders. OCR is not perfect and does not prove that a screenshot is authentic. This page currently stores evidence in the private bucket but does not yet attach it to a specific agreement or review record.
